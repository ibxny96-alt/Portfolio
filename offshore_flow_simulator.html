The governing physics

The flow is governed by the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations:

∇·u = 0
∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u = −(1/ρ)∇p + ν∇²u

A conventional neural network would only fit CFD samples. A physics-informed network additionally forces its own predictions to satisfy these equations, using automatic differentiation to compute the residual at points where no CFD data exists.

Physics-informed loss

L = λ₁ L_data + λ₂ L_physics + λ₃ L_boundary
  • L_data — mismatch against labelled CFD samples
  • L_physics — how badly the network violates the Navier–Stokes residual at sampled collocation points
  • L_boundary — mismatch against known wall / inlet / outlet conditions

Why it's useful here

  • Full CFD per design case: hours. Trained surrogate inference: a fraction of a second — try the mode switch on the Simulator tab.
  • Physics constraints mean fewer expensive CFD runs are needed to train a generalisable model.
  • Enables near real-time design screening and digital-twin monitoring of offshore structures.
