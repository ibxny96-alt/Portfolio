03 HOW I WORK / FROM QUESTION TO SIMULATION

Physics, put
to code.

The approach behind my projects: take a physical phenomenon, write down the physics, and turn it into something you can run, test and learn from.

01 / Question↘

Pick a phenomenon

Choose something physical worth understanding—a fold, a wake, a current path, a lift force—and ask what governs it.

02 / Model↗

Write down the physics

Identify the forces, equations and assumptions, and decide what the model needs to capture.

03 / Simulate↗

Turn it into code

Build an interactive simulation or calculator that makes the idea visible and lets anyone test it.

04 / Verify↗

Check it against reality

Compare results with known physics, note where the model breaks down, and let the next question emerge.
